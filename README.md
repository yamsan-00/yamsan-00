## 💰 Funded Project

### 🌟 Topological Quantum Error Correction for NISQ Devices
**Research & Development Cell, D Y Patil International University** | *Apr 2026 – Present* | Pune, India

<div align="center">

| Aspect | Details |
|--------|---------|
| **Objective** | Develop Variational Quantum Error Decoder (VQED) for surface codes |
| **Methodology** | Integrate surface codes with variational quantum algorithms using parameterized quantum circuits |
| **Target** | 10–30% improvement in error rates vs. standard MWPM decoders |
| **Hardware** | Real quantum hardware (NISQ devices) |
| **Funding** | University R&D Grant |

</div>

<details>
<summary><b>🔬 Technical Approach (click to expand)</b></summary>

- **Surface Codes:** Topological quantum error correction codes with high threshold
- **Variational Quantum Algorithms:** Parameterized quantum circuits optimized classically
- **VQED Architecture:** Hybrid quantum-classical decoder replacing Minimum Weight Perfect Matching (MWPM)
- **Optimization:** Gradient-based parameter updates using quantum hardware feedback
- **Benchmarking:** Comparison against MWPM on IBM Quantum / Rigetti devices

</details>
